# Cadence scoreboard lineup — what ships and why

Decided from measured data, not guesswork. Rows per app are real result
rows in the shipped datasets.

## Ships

| App | Real rows | Span | Why it earns a slot |
|-----|-----------|------|---------------------|
| DCI | 63,015 | 53 seasons, live | The flagship: dense competition, live season, full caption recaps |
| BOA | 19,609 | 49 seasons (1976–2025) + captions | Deepest marching-band archive online; event-shaped board |
| UIL Texas | 24,745 | 47 seasons, 1,742 bands | Whole-state coverage; Division ratings, never faked as scores |
| TCGC | 15,326 | 13 seasons + captions | Real winter circuit with judge-level sheets |
| FFCC | 11,331 | 16 seasons + captions | Guard, percussion and winds in one circuit |
| WGASC | 9,515 | 5 seasons + captions | Largest SoCal winter circuit |
| US Bands | 2,645 | 2 seasons | Real but thin — backfill is the obvious next win |
| WGI ×3 | 0 scores | champions history + 2027 schedule | Separate apps by activity (guard / percussion / winds) — they are
  distinct categories with distinct audiences. Scores sit behind WGI's
  directors' portal; the board shows champions history until permission
  lands. |

## Retired

| App | Why |
|-----|-----|
| ISSMA | 1,965 placement rows across one event a year, and no scores at all
  (ISSMA keeps them directors-only). One thin annual result set doesn't earn a
  top-level destination. `/issma/` redirects to the app picker; the scraper and
  research notes stay in the repo if it's ever worth revisiting. |

## Note on workspaces

Cadence Ensemble is deliberately *not* limited to the circuits above. Any
marching organization can create a workspace and pick from 11 organization
types — drum corps, marching band, winter guard, indoor percussion, booster,
school program and more. Linking a workspace to a public scoreboard is an
optional bonus, so score coverage and workspace coverage never have to match.
