1. What Cadence is

Cadence is two things:

  • Public scoreboards. Free, public pages showing marching-arts competition scores, schedules, standings, and history. No account is needed and no account will ever be needed to see a score.
  • Cadence Ensemble. Private, paid workspaces for organizations — announcements, chat, calendar and attendance, file storage, forms, and drill tools. Organizations pay for a workspace; individual members never pay to belong to one.

These draft terms do not yet name a legal entity. The final terms will identify the person or entity operating Cadence. Until then, "Cadence" and "we" mean the operator of the Cadence site.

2. Accounts

  • Cadence accounts are passwordless. You sign in with your email address and a one-time link or code sent to that address; Cadence stores no password for you.
  • Because your email inbox is your key, you are responsible for keeping that inbox secure. Tell your organization and use the contact address published on the Cadence site if you believe your account has been used without your permission.
  • You agree to use an email address you actually control and to keep it current.
  • Minors participate in Cadence Ensemble only through an organization that invites them, subject to the organization's settings and the practices described on the youth safety page.

3. Organization workspaces

  • A workspace is created and administered by an organization (a school program, independent ensemble, circuit group, or similar). The person who creates it represents that they are authorized to act for that organization.
  • The organization decides who is invited, what roles and permissions members hold, and how the workspace's safety settings are configured. For its workspace content, the organization is the controller; Cadence is the platform that stores and serves it under the organization's settings.
  • The organization is responsible for its members' use of the workspace, including compliance with its own policies and any rules that apply to it (school district, circuit, or league policies among them).

4. Subscriptions and trial

  • Every new workspace starts with a 60-day free trial with no payment details required.
  • When a trial or subscription ends, the workspace becomes read-only: nothing new can be created, but existing content is preserved and remains visible to its members. Content is not deleted because a subscription lapsed.
  • Current prices are published on the Cadence site. Price changes will not affect an already-paid period.
  • Billing is not live. Checkout currently runs only in a payment provider's test mode; no real money can change hands anywhere in Cadence today. Before any organization is actually charged, Cadence will complete legal review of these terms and publish the final version.

5. Acceptable use

You agree not to use Cadence to:

  • harass, threaten, or harm anyone, or attempt to contact a minor in violation of an organization's safety settings;
  • upload content you do not have the right to share (see Section 7);
  • probe, bypass, or interfere with access controls, rate limits, or the database permission rules that protect other people's data;
  • misrepresent competition results or present fabricated scores as real;
  • scrape or bulk-harvest personal data of other members;
  • break any applicable law.

6. Your content and ownership

  • Organizations and their members own the content they put into a workspace — posts, messages, files, forms, drill, all of it.
  • Cadence receives only the limited, non-exclusive license needed to operate the service: to store your content, transmit it to the people your organization's settings allow to see it, back it up, and display it inside the product. That license ends when the content is deleted, except for short-lived backups.
  • Cadence does not sell your content, does not use it for advertising, and claims no ownership of it.

7. Music and file uploads

  • The person who uploads a file is responsible for having the rights to share it with the workspace members who can see it. This matters most for sheet music, arrangements, and audio recordings, which are usually under copyright and often require licenses your organization must obtain itself.
  • Cadence grants no music-performance, arrangement, synchronization, or distribution rights of any kind. Storing a file in Cadence does not license it.
  • Rights holders may send takedown requests to the contact address published on the Cadence site; Cadence honors them. A formal notice procedure will be defined during pre-launch legal review.

8. Termination

  • Cadence may suspend or terminate accounts or workspaces that violate these terms, with notice where practical. Where the violation is not a safety or legal emergency, organizations will be given the opportunity to export their data first.
