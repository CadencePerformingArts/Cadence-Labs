# Cadence family — design & bug audit

**Date:** 2026-08-07 · **Method:** Playwright (Chromium) against `docs/` served locally, viewports **390×844** and **1280×800**, all 6 apps (`/`, `/wgi/guard/`, `/wgi/percussion/`, `/wgi/winds/`, `/boa/`, `/usbands/`) × 7 routes (`#/`, `#/events`, `#/corps`, `#/data`, `#/champions`, `#/records`, `#/settings`) = **84 page loads**, plus `#/database` and deep corps-profile loads on BOA, plus click-tests (mode switcher, standings row → profile, fifth tab). Screenshots in `data/audit-shots/`.

**Overall sweep result:** no page errors, no literal `${` leaks, no `NaN` in visible text, no horizontal overflow, no broken images on any of the 84 pages. The two real defects found are behavioral (below). Note: `docs/boa/data/` was rewritten by the live BOA scraper *mid-audit*, which surfaced a genuine empty-data edge case (issue 2).

## Prioritized polish list (premium feel, keeps the shared DCI look)

1. **Fix the fifth-tab `undefined` href** (issue 1) — a one-key change in the engine build script; a dead flagship tab is the single biggest premium-killer.
2. **Empty-state guard on Champions** (issue 2) — never show `undefined`; a warm “first season in progress” card keeps trust during live ingests.
3. **Terminology sweep of the family engine** (issue 3) — grep generated copy for hardcoded “corps”; every leaked word breaks the illusion that each circuit got its own app.
4. **Chart badge leader lines + sparse-series handling** (issue 4) — BOA’s few-events-per-season shape is the live-data future for USBands/state circuits too; charts must look intentional with 2-point series.
5. **One interstitial max on first visit** (issue 5) — let the scoreboard make the first impression; recap and install prompts can wait a beat.
6. **Center/curate desktop Settings** (issue 7) — cheapest big win for perceived finish on wide screens.
7. **Hue-distance fallback for team-color chart lines** (issue 8) — keeps team identity where it works, legibility where it doesn't.
8. **Fade backdrop under sticky Show-All pills** (issue 10).
9. **Consistent event-state chip placement** (issue 11) and label the duplicated per-judge caption chips (issue 6).
10. **Bottom-nav label fit at 390px** (issue 9) — tighten label tracking or pill padding so all five tabs breathe.

*Everything above is additive polish inside the existing navy/gold system — no layout or brand changes required. Fixes to `docs/family/app.js` must go through `scripts/build_family_engine.py` (the file header says generated; direct edits will be overwritten on deploy).*
